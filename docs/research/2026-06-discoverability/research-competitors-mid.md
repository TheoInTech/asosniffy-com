## SUMMARY
Scraped pricing pages for all six assigned mid-market ASO tools on 2026-06-10 (Checkaso's domain checkaso.io is DNS-dead — no A/NS records — so it appears defunct). The five live tools are uniformly seat-based monthly subscriptions: Appfigures $9.99-$399.99/mo (popularity scores gated at $44.99, competitor tracking with just ONE competitor at $149.99, keyword-level revenue estimates at $299.99), Asodesk $53-$404/mo (keyword translations and full exports gated at $179, review replies sold as a separate $44-$260/mo product), App Radar EUR 69-299/mo with hard quotas even on AI (50-200 GPT-4 replies, 50-100 AI summaries per month), AppFollow ~$179 Essential/$599 Team with prices hidden behind a JS calculator, and FoxData with fully JS-hidden prices but the only publicly documented keyword methodology (notably defining 'Popularity' as Apple Search Ads bid frequency — validating Sniffy's existing ASA integration as a popularity signal). On agent accessibility, the verdict is stark: no tool offers per-call pricing, crypto, MCP, or agent-completable signup; App Radar's FAQ explicitly routes API access through enterprise sales, FoxData's API requires a consultation form, and Asodesk's pricing page doesn't mention an API at all — Appfigures is the friendliest (API bundled with cheap plans, public docs) but still account-and-card bound. The dominant 2025-2026 product trend is AI review management (AppFollow processed 10B OpenAI tokens and automates 50% of replies; App Radar and FoxData both ship GPT-based review summaries/replies) and ASO+ASA convergence. Indie sentiment on Reddit anchors acceptable spend at $10-70/mo, complains Appfigures gets expensive fast, and includes a notable 'ASO tools are dead' disillusionment thread. The clearest openings for Sniffy: per-call price anchoring, a documented popularity proxy, quota-free review and competitor analysis per report, promoting the already-built localization gap analysis against $179/mo translation gates, and converting its structural agent-buyability monopoly into distribution via discovery listings.
