## SUMMARY
LLM-era app discoverability is now a measurable, monetizable layer that sits upstream of app-store conversion, and the tooling for it is brand-new and almost entirely agent-hostile. Ground truth on what gets an app named by ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini: it is intent-driven shortlist synthesis built on (a) training-set association between the app name and use cases and (b) live web retrieval — app-store rank is NOT a direct input (AppTweak, Feb 2026), while domain authority (32k+ referring domains = 3.5x citation odds), Reddit/Quora mention volume (4x citation lift), fact-dense 'answer-block' content, and presence in comparison listicles are the dominant signals. A whole 'AI visibility' tool category has formed for web brands (Profound $99-$499/mo, Peec ~$100/mo, Otterly $29-$489/mo, Ahrefs Brand Radar $199-$699/mo) but every one of them is a dashboard subscription with no agent-buyable API. Exactly one vendor has moved to mobile apps: AppTweak launched 'AI Visibility for Apps' in April 2026 (10,000+ prompts, intents, position map, sentiment, source attribution) — demo-gated, enterprise-priced, dashboard-only. Two genuinely new OS/assistant distribution surfaces opened in the last six months: the ChatGPT App Directory (Dec 2025, submissions require an MCP server; app NAME carries most indexation weight; 800-900M WAU) and Apple's WWDC26 Siri AI / App Schemas (June 8, 2026), where App Intents + IndexedEntity literally determine whether an app's content is 'discoverable by Apple Intelligence.' For Sniffy, the open lane is clear: a per-request, x402-payable 'AI share-of-voice' probe (run canonical intent prompts against LLM APIs, report app-vs-competitor mention rate, position, sentiment) plus a citation-source footprint audit (Reddit, listicles, comparison pages) — both buildable from cheap LLM API calls and free/cheap public data, both unpriced for indie devs, and both recurring (40-60% of AI-cited domains churn monthly, so re-scans have natural repeat-purchase value).

## FINDINGS
- [high] AI app recommendations are intent-driven shortlists built from model training knowledge plus live web retrieval — app store rankings are not a direct input.
  AppTweak's February 2026 analysis states AI engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) rely on two primary inputs: training-set associations between app names and use cases, and live web retrieval of relevant passages. App store rankings 'are not directly used as a primary decision input,' though strong store performance indirectly helps via broader web coverage — AI recommendations and store rank are 'correlated but not identical.' The discovery flow is: user asks AI, gets a curated shortlist, then opens the store to confirm before installing — so AI sits upstream of store conversion, shaping the consideration set before ASO ever applies.
  src: https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/ai-app-discovery-llm-search
- [medium] Domain authority acts as a hard 'trust cliff' for ChatGPT citations: sites with 32,000+ referring domains are ~3.5x more likely to be cited, with an 'invisible floor' around 190,000 monthly visitors.
  SE Ranking's 2025 analysis of 400,000 URLs found a non-linear trust threshold: below ~32k referring domains citation rates are flat, above it they jump 3.5x; a secondary traffic floor of ~190k monthly visits shows the same step function. Implication for indie apps: their own websites will almost never be cited directly — visibility must come through high-authority third parties (review sites, Reddit, listicles) that clear the threshold. Reported via Yotpo's 2026 GEO guide which links the underlying SE Ranking studies.
  src: https://seranking.com/blog/ranking-factors-for-chatgpt/
- [medium] High mention volume on Reddit and Quora correlates with a 4x increase in ChatGPT citations, and pages with 120-180 word sections perform best as citation sources.
  LLMs use cross-source 'consensus' to suppress hallucination, so community platforms function as human reinforcement of facts: if a tool is frequently recommended on relevant subreddits, the model raises its probability weight in its own recommendations. The same study found content chunked into 120-180 word sections aligns with AI answer paragraph size and is cited more. This makes Reddit-thread presence a directly measurable, public, app-level GEO signal.
  src: https://www.digitalinformationworld.com/2025/11/how-chatgpt-decides-what-to-cite-new.html
- [medium] The Princeton GEO study found 'fact density' (citations, statistics, quotations) can boost lower-ranked sites' visibility in AI answers by up to 40%, while keyword stuffing has negligible or negative effect.
  The original GEO paper (arXiv 2311.09735) tested optimization tactics against generative engines and found structural/factual signals beat classic SEO tactics — challenger brands can win citations without winning the link graph. Caveat: this is a November 2023 paper (pre-2024), so treat the exact 40% figure as directional; the 2025-2026 industry studies (SE Ranking, Zenith) broadly corroborate the direction.
  src: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.09735
- [medium] ChatGPT cites competitor-owned comparison pages 11.1 points more than Google does, making 'X vs Y' and 'alternative to X' pages a disproportionately effective GEO surface.
  Zenith AI's citation study found that unlike Google (which favors neutral third parties for comparison queries), ChatGPT goes straight to vendor-owned comparison content when synthesizing 'best/vs' answers — provided the content reads as objective technical documentation rather than marketing spin. For app makers this means a plain-prose comparison page on their own site is one of the few owned assets that can win an AI citation.
  src: https://www.tryzenith.ai/blog/chatgpt-citation-study
- [high] AppTweak launched 'AI Visibility for Apps' (April 2026) — the first and so far only AI-search visibility platform built specifically for mobile apps — and it is demo-gated, dashboard-only, with no public pricing or API.
  The product tracks how often ChatGPT recommends an app across 10,000+ curated prompts organized into thousands of intents across 200+ app subcategories derived from app-store behavior (not web prompts). Features: AI visibility score, intent performance (which user goals you are/aren't recommended for), competitor position map, per-intent sentiment, and source attribution (which sites/communities shape AI's view of the app). A games edition shipped May 2026. Access is 'Book a demo' enterprise sales — no self-serve, no API, no per-request option, nothing an AI agent could buy programmatically.
  src: https://www.apptweak.com/en/ai-search-visibility-tool-for-mobile-apps
- [high] Brand-level AI visibility tools are priced $29-$499/mo at entry/mid tiers and are uniformly subscription dashboards: Profound Starter $99/mo (ChatGPT only), Profound Lite $499/mo (4 engines), Otterly $29/$189/$489, AthenaHQ $295/mo, Ahrefs Brand Radar $199/platform or $699 bundle, Semrush AI Toolkit $99/mo.
  Friction AI's April 2026 landscape comparison (12+ tools, 4 categories) shows the whole category is seat-license SaaS: entry $29-$99/mo, mid-tier $189-$499/mo, enterprise custom (Conductor averages ~$61K/year). None advertise an API-first or pay-per-query product; the article notes most tools 'monitor only' and stop at diagnosis. The category 'looks like the SEO tooling market in 2010' with consolidation expected by 2028. None of these target mobile apps as the entity being tracked.
  src: https://www.frictionai.co/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools-compared-2026
- [high] Peec AI starts at $100/mo, tracks ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews daily via UI scraping (not APIs), charges €20-30/mo extra per additional engine (Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek), and hit €650K ARR within four months of its early-2025 launch.
  Peec measures share of voice and citation rate per prompt set, distinguishing brand mentions from direct source-link citations, with competitive benchmarking and source attribution. Its UI-scraping methodology is explicitly chosen to capture the same responses real users see rather than API-based estimates — a relevant methodological choice if Sniffy probes via API instead (cheaper but slightly different distribution). The review also relays Profound's finding that 40-60% of AI-cited domains change monthly ('citation drift'), which is the argument for continuous/repeat measurement.
  src: https://discoveredlabs.com/blog/peec-ai-review-best-for-ai-visibility-monitoring-use-cases-limits-alternatives
- [high] OpenAI opened ChatGPT app submissions on December 17, 2025, with an in-product app directory (chatgpt.com/apps); submission requires an MCP server, directory metadata, and country availability settings.
  Apps extend ChatGPT conversations (order groceries, search apartments) and get triggered when @-mentioned, picked from the tools menu, or — critically for discoverability — proactively surfaced by OpenAI 'using signals like conversational context, app usage patterns, and user preferences.' Submissions go through platform.openai.com/apps-manage and include MCP connectivity details and testing guidelines; approved apps also become Codex plugins. This is a brand-new metadata-governed discovery surface with its own (currently primitive) ranking system.
  src: https://openai.com/index/developers-can-now-submit-apps-to-chatgpt/
- [high] Phiture's ASO Stack analysis (March 2026) of the ChatGPT App Directory: the app NAME carries the most indexation weight, long-tail keyword search doesn't reliably work yet, and keyword stuffing risks rejection — and ChatGPT's 800M WAU now exceeds the Apple App Store's 650M.
  Phiture (a top ASO agency) treats the directory as a legitimate new ASO surface: submission requires icon, name, descriptions, screenshots, a verified website, and an MCP server; early movers are Spotify, Adobe Acrobat, Canva, Booking.com, MyFitnessPal, AllTrails; Stripe-powered Instant Checkout enables in-chat commerce for US users (digital services excluded). Note a source conflict on scale: Friction AI cites Search Engine Land putting ChatGPT at 900M WAU by February 2026 vs Phiture's 800M — directionally consistent, both above the App Store's reported 650M.
  src: https://phiture.com/asostack/chat-gpt-app-directory/
- [high] Apple's WWDC26 (June 8, 2026) made App Intents the explicit gateway to 'Siri AI' discoverability: App Entities, IndexedEntity, schema domains, semantic search, and onscreen awareness determine whether an app's content and actions are surfaced by Apple Intelligence.
  Apple's press release says App Intents framework updates 'enable developers to connect their apps to Siri AI capabilities like personal context understanding, app actions, and onscreen awareness' to make content 'more discoverable and accessible across the system.' The WWDC26 session 'Build intelligent Siri experiences with App Schemas' covers entity resolution, IndexedEntity for Spotlight semantic search, and adopting schema domains; companion docs are literally titled 'Making actions and content discoverable by Apple Intelligence' and 'Making app entities available in Spotlight.' This is OS-level AI legibility as a concrete, auditable developer checklist — two days old as of this research.
  src: https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/apple-aids-app-development-with-new-intelligence-frameworks-and-advanced-tools/
- [medium] The macro shift is quantified: Gartner predicts 25% decline in traditional search volume by 2026, zero-click searches reached ~60% of queries in 2025, and OpenAI/Harvard (NBER w34255) found information-seeking is 24% of all ChatGPT interactions.
  These three data points (relayed with primary links in Yotpo's 2026 GEO guide) establish that the 'ask an AI which app to get' behavior is mainstream, not fringe. The proposed new KPI for the category is 'citation rate' — frequency with which a brand is used as a source in AI answers — replacing CTR. For app developers this means a measurable share of installs now originates in a channel no ASO keyword tool observes.
  src: https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-02-19-gartner-predicts-search-engine-volume-will-drop-25-percent-by-2026-due-to-ai-chatbots-and-other-virtual-agents
- [high] GEO/AEO is now mainstream-press validated: the Wall Street Journal covered companies 'spending big on generative engine optimization' to manipulate ChatGPT results.
  Christopher Mims's WSJ piece ('How Businesses Are Manipulating ChatGPT Results') documents that GEO/AEO spend is a real budget line, not a fringe SEO fad. This matters for Sniffy positioning: indie hackers are reading about GEO in mainstream press but have zero affordable tooling to measure it for their app — the existing tools target web brands at $99-499/mo or mobile enterprises via AppTweak demos.
  src: https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/ai-what-is-geo-aeo-5c452500
